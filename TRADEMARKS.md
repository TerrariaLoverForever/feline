## What's fine

- Saying your project is "based on Feline" or "a fork of Feline".
- Distributing **unmodified** Feline releases under the Feline name, including

- Using the name to refer to the project in articles, reviews and tutorials.

## What needs a different name and icon

- Distributing a **modified** version of Feline. Give it its own name and icon
  so users aren't confused about who made it.
